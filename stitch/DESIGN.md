## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the principles of quiet luxury, tactile minimalism, and understated elegance tailored for a high-end direct-to-consumer mobile flagship. Designed specifically for sensory categories—haute perfumery, bespoke jewelry, and ritual skincare—the interface steps back to let the physical craft, formulations, and imagery command absolute focus.

### Tone & Ethos
- **Discreet Refinement:** Absence of loud marketing tropes, urgency tickers, or harsh conversion prompts. The commerce experience feels curated, unhurried, and intimate.
- **Architectural Poise:** Rooted in disciplined proportions, generous negative space, and deliberate typographic rhythm.
- **Materiality:** Translates the physical experience of high-grade paper stock, brushed brass, and cold stone into soft ivory containers, hairline separations, and champagne accents.

### Visual Style
- **Minimalism with Tactile Warmth:** Eliminates synthetic drop shadows and heavy overlays in favor of subtle ivory tonal shifts, alabaster backgrounds, and warm hairline dividers.
- **Micro-Detailing:** Micro-interactions are subtle, featuring low-friction fade transitions, soft borders, and tracked uppercase utility typography.

## Colors

The palette avoids stark sterile whites and absolute digital blacks. Instead, it relies on warm, pigment-inspired tones reminiscent of archival paper, raw plaster, and refined metalwork.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#2A2A2A`):** Deep Charcoal. Serves as the principal visual anchor for primary typography, iconography, and dominant interactive touchpoints. Replaces raw black to soften optical harshness against off-white surfaces.
- **Secondary Accent (`#C8A97E`):** Soft Champagne Gold. Used with surgical precision for active state indicators, subtle badges, bespoke accents, and selected micro-copy. Never used for massive flooded fills.
- **Tertiary Container (`#F5F3EF`):** Warm Ivory. Used as a structural contrasting tier for product card backplates, elevated sheets, input fields, and carousel cells.
- **Canvas Neutral (`#FAFAF8`):** Warm Off-White / Alabaster. The foundational backdrop across the entire application, imparting warmth and an editorial feel.
- **Text Muted (`#737373`):** Neutral Mid-Tone. Applied to metadata, secondary specifications, volume disclosures (e.g., "50 ml / 1.7 fl oz"), and inactive controls.
- **Structural Border (`#EAE6DF`):** Hairline Warm Gray / Soft Linen. Defines cards, input outlines, and dividers without visually segmenting the interface.

## Typography

The typographical structure is built exclusively upon **Inter**, leveraged through nuanced weights, open line-heights, and architectural letter-spacing.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Character
- **Headlines & Display:** Set in Light (`300`) and Regular (`400`) weights with tight tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to deliver modern, quiet editorial confidence rather than commercial loudness.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Rendered in small, uppercase formats with wide letter-spacing (`+0.14em` to `+0.16em`). Used for category tags, olfactory notes, metal karats, status markers, and step flows.
- **Body & Editorial Copy:** Balanced at `14px` and `16px` with generous line-height ratios (`1.6x`) to facilitate comfortable reading of ingredient provenance, fragrance stories, and craft documentation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on a mobile-first fluid baseline with calm pacing and clear spatial intervals.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Margins & Gutter Logic:** Mobile viewports operate on a strict 4-column fluid grid, utilizing a fixed `1.25rem` (`20px`) canvas margin to maximize product presentation while preserving breathing room. Column gutters rest at `1rem` (`16px`) for standard flows and `0.5rem` (`8px`) for tight dual-product lookbooks.
- **Generous Section Stride:** Sections (e.g., Product Editorial into Olfactory Notes into Ingredients) are spaced with `space-2xl` (`3.5rem` / `56px`), maintaining distinct thematic blocks without visually cluttered divider components.
- **Vertical Alignment:** Components follow an 8pt structural rhythm, with micro-elements adhering strictly to a 4pt sub-grid.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve the quiet luxury aesthetic, heavy shadows and elevated drop-planes are avoided. Physical depth is expressed through tonal stacking and precise hairlines.

### Surface Tiers
1. **Foundation (Level 0):** Alabaster base canvas (`#FAFAF8`).
2. **Contained Surface (Level 1):** Subtle Warm Ivory (`#F5F3EF`) or Pure Linen White (`#FFFFFF`) framed with a 1px border of `#EAE6DF`.
3. **Floating Overlays & Modals (Level 2):** Bottom drawers (Quick Add, Cart Slide, Bag Drawer) retain a solid White (`#FFFFFF`) surface with an ambient, heavily diffused contact blur:
   - `box-shadow: 0 -8px 24px -6px rgba(42, 42, 42, 0.04), 0 -1px 0 0 #EAE6DF`
4. **Frosted Navigation Glass:** Sticky top navigation and floating mobile bar use `#FAFAF8` with `92%` opacity paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a subtle `0.5px` bottom border of `#EAE6DF`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework is defined by sharp corners (`roundedness: 0`), evoking architectural tailoring, museum framing, and classic luxury packaging.

### Shape Guidelines
- **Containers & Cards:** Strictly sharp `0px` radius. All product cards, lifestyle hero modules, bottom sheets, and dialogs feature pristine right-angle edges.
- **Inputs & CTAs:** Pure rectangular silhouettes. Form fields and buttons rely on crisp perimeter borders rather than rounded radii.
- **Visual Balance:** Sharp geometry is paired with soft off-white surface tones, fine hairline borders, and generous internal padding (`space-md` to `space-lg`) to ensure touchpoints feel refined rather than harsh.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#2A2A2A`, text `#FAFAF8`, sharp `0px` edges. Height: `48px`. Typography: `label-caps-lg` (uppercase, tracked). Active state: subtle opacity drop to `0.9` with no transform shift.
- **Secondary / Outlined:** Transparent background, `1px` solid `#2A2A2A` border, text `#2A2A2A`.
- **Text Action:** Clean inline button with `1px` underline offset by `4px`, transitioning to `#C8A97E` on interaction.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Inputs:** Height `50px`, surface `#FFFFFF` or `#F5F3EF`, enclosed with `1px` border of `#EAE6DF`. Floating or inline label set in `label-caps-sm` with color `#737373`. Active focus swaps border to `#2A2A2A` without glowing drop shadows.
- **Variant Selectors (Volume, Finish, Ring Size):** Minimalist rectangular tiles. Default: `#FAFAF8` with `#EAE6DF` border. Selected: `#2A2A2A` fill with `#FAFAF8` text, or subtle underline accent in `#C8A97E`.

### Cards & Merchandising
- **Product Card:** Borderless or framed with a `1px` hairline `#EAE6DF`. Primary image uses a calm `3:4` or `1:1` ratio over a `#F5F3EF` background wash. Title set in `headline-sm`, subtitle/size in `body-sm` (`#737373`), and price in `body-md` (`#2A2A2A`).
- **Quick-Add Trigger:** Embedded unobtrusively at the base of the card as a subtle bottom-bar touch target or gentle `+` icon rather than an aggressive, contrasting button.

### Selection Controls
- **Radio & Checkbox:** Sharp rectangular indicators (`16x16px`) with a `1px` border of `#737373`. Selected state features a filled `#2A2A2A` center without rounded corners.

### Lists & Accordions
- **Editorial Accordions (Fragrance Pyramid, Ritual, Ingredients):** Divided by `1px` horizontal lines in `#EAE6DF`. Header row uses `label-caps-lg` with an understated `+` and `−` glyph toggle. Expanded content flows smoothly in `body-md` typography.

### Badges & Micro-Labels
- **Product Tags (e.g., "LIMITED RUN", "EXTRACT"):** Background transparent, enclosed in `1px` border of `#C8A97E`, text in `#C8A97E` set using `label-caps-sm`.
